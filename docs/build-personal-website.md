# Visuals

First, the color scheme will be predominantly red. 
The background of the website should be a deep red, #7e111a, though feel free to choose a red that will not be too harsh and will contrast well with the page contents.
This will be the primary color of the color scheme. 
There should probably be two secondary colors, one of which should be white (or an off white if contrasts better) and the other I propose an apricot though feel free to select another color.

For font, I want to use one single font throughout. 
I was envisioning Garamond though you are welcome to change this to something adjacent or even entirely different.
The website contents should also have a strong general middle center alignment throughout.

Overall, the website should follow a minimalist and modern style with relatively little decorative UI. 
There should be lots of whitespace and padding on the page contents to emphasize the empty space and keep things light, simple and clean. 
It should be motion-driven, relying on smooth transitions and interactive animations to give fluidness to the page. 
There should be a throughline of sophistication without visual clutter.
